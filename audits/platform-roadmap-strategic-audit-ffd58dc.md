## Verdicts

- **Q1 - materially incomplete operationally.** Coverage is substantially complete across MVP, product-enabling, and mature-platform frames, but the causal learning chain and autonomous executor are not operating (PRA-01, PRA-02).
- **Q2 - advanced.** The roadmap has strong typed-tool, bounded-authority, independent-verification, and governed-delivery design. ## Feature Completeness

The **MVP frame** has an executable design but not an operating base path. T4.1/T4.2, the queue producer, SHA-bound callback, persona contracts, and loop budgets remain unbuilt. Human-driven `/orient -> /plan -> /implement` plus governed CI/CD is a strong safety fallback, but it does not meet the prompt's no-required-human-transition definition (PRA-02).

The **product-enabling frame** names platform dependencies and GAP markers, which is a genuine strength. TCA, telemetry analysis, and recursive-loop dependencies remain unbuilt, while cross-roadmap completion proof is weaker than the within-roadmap schema discipline (PRA-01, PRA-06).

The **mature-platform frame** owns most P1-P8 capabilities, but causal outcome evidence, behavioral model/prompt regression, pre-live injection containment, and migration retirement are not operational (PRA-01, PRA-03, PRA-04, PRA-05).

## Frontier Practice

The roadmap is architecturally advanced: narrow typed verbs, precision context, SLOC-based model portability, GitHub Actions as independent verification authority, autonomy gates, least-authority deployment roles, and explicit degraded paths are unusually coherent. It appropriately prefers calibrated autonomy over maximum autonomy.

It is not operationally frontier. A system can still report successful actions and checks while outcome quality worsens because T2.36/T3.2/T3.3/T3.4 are unbuilt (PRA-01). T4.14 owns offline persona regression but is unbuilt (PRA-03). T4.13 recognizes prompt injection but currently follows T4.2, permitting first-live persona work before its threat model and adversarial fixtures (PRA-04).

## Improvements and Simplifications

1. **PRA-01:** close operational data migration, causal verification, cloud analysis, and control-plane evaluation before expanding autonomy.
2. **PRA-04:** move T4.13 before or inside the first T4.2 persona slice. Least authority helps, but it does not prevent instruction confusion from logs, recommendations, diffs, or repository text.
3. **PRA-05:** complete table disposition and retire ops_compaction and obsolete Athena/staging concepts. Preserve the warehouse/cache invariant and do not introduce another migration service.
4. **PRA-06:** validate cross-roadmap PLATFORM references against status and exit evidence at product eligibility time.

The simplification is property-preserving: one writer/read boundary, one maintenance mechanism per live table, and the governed deployment lifecycle remain. Temporary restore and migration validation cost is justified by the net removal.

## Develop First

1. **T2.26 -> T2.36 (PRA-05):** establish one data and maintenance path.
2. **T3.2 -> T3.3 -> T3.4 (PRA-01):** prove outcome-linked learning, including negative controls.
3. **T4.13 + T4.10a + T4.11 (PRA-04):** establish containment, persona authority, and bounded loops.
4. **T4.3 + T4.9a + T4.1 + T4.2 (PRA-02):** close one sandbox autonomous iteration at concurrency one.
5. **T4.14 before T4.4 promotion (PRA-03):** measure behavioral quality before authority rises.

Skipping steps 1-2 leaves causal closure impossible; skipping step 3 makes first-live authority insufficiently contained; skipping step 4 leaves MVP autonomy absent; skipping step 5 leaves model and prompt regressions invisible.

## Assumptions That Reframe the Roadmap

The sequence assumes low-scale, latency-tolerant operation; affordable model/cloud calls; stable public-repository and AWS choices; identifiable telemetry effects; tolerable human review; and stable product demands. The most consequential reversal is non-identifiable outcomes: then T3.4 cannot support causal improvement, Q2 falls, and executor expansion should defer. Persistent freeze or model regression similarly moves interactive evaluation ahead of orchestration. Regulatory expansion would add pre-live controls and lower operational completeness. If migration overhead dominates sole-developer capacity, PRA-05 becomes the immediate priority.

## Questions Not Asked

- **What falsifies the north star?** A negative-control study where every gate passes but outcome quality declines (PRA-01).
- **What should never be automated?** Unbounded authority changes, bootstrap-admin mutation, or treating untrusted content as policy (PRA-04).
- **What recurring cost is inherited?** Parallel migration maintenance, workflow monitoring, evidence review, and provider drift (PRA-05).
- **What triggers redesign?** Persistently non-identifiable outcomes, model regressions, regulatory expansion, or autonomous economics worse than the interactive path (PRA-01, PRA-03).
